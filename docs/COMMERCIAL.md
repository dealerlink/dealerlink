## Why this file exists

F.174's own filing states the problem, and it is not "we have no price":

> Every technical decision taken in the last fortnight has had a commercial
> premise underneath it, and those premises have been settled ad hoc. F.55's

document that pays for itself. The rest is scaffolding for the operator's
content.

**Corrected 2026-10-03 — the three bullets here were written against the

> - **Phase 1 is not the moment they switch invoicing systems.** … "Do not sell
>   Phase 1 as the moment they switch. It isn't."
> - **The Phase 1 case therefore rests on two things only** — stock that cannot go
>   negative, and serials that can be traced.

  `CLIENT_CONTEXT.md` §4, both corrected the same day). `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` has
  been corrected and now carries the cutover framing.
- **THE CASE IS THE WHOLE SALE IN ONE SYSTEM**, not two features. Stock that
  cannot go negative and traceable serials are still the sharpest demonstrations
  — they are what their own screenshots show failing — but the offer is quotation
  through tax invoice, with the ledger fix behind it, rather than a partial
  system bought for two of its parts.
- **Two real costs to name, and neither is double entry:** about ten minutes a day
  of manual IRN generation until F.24, and **migrating their history, whose
  extraction route from Swipe is not established** (F.179). Say the second is
  unquantified rather than small.
- **The Tally ledger fix is no longer gated on a phase that was years away.** It
  is gated on the cutover and on the Tally block landing (days 54–77). Still **do
